## 👋 The person behind the platforms

**I connect business ambition with the engineering that makes it possible.**

I'm Nehemiah, a **Solutions Architect and AWS Community Builder** from Kenya. With a background in software engineering, I work across cloud architecture, enterprise integration, and DevOps—turning complex requirements into systems people can build, run, and grow.

I care about thoughtful design, dependable delivery, and making technical ideas easier to understand. You'll find me building on AWS, exploring Kubernetes, automating workflows, and sharing what I learn along the way.
